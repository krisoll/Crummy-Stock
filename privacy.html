Privacy Policy

CrummyStock is a local-first shop inventory and sales app. It has no accounts, no cloud sync, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Your shop data stays on your device unless you choose to export or transfer it.

Effective date: November 7, 2026 Last updated: November 7, 2026 Version: 1.0 App: CrummyStock (Android) Package: com.krisoll.crummystock
English Español

The short version

  • We do not collect your shop data. Products, sales, suppliers, photos, and settings are stored on your Android device.
  • No account is required. There is no sign-up, login, cloud sync, or remote server.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking, profiling, or crash reporting. The app does not use advertising IDs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data. We do not have access to the data on your device.
  • You decide when data leaves the app. Backups, CSV/Excel exports, and same-Wi-Fi transfers only happen when you start them.
  • You can delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and by deleting any backup files you created.

1. Who we are

This privacy policy is issued by Krisoll, an individual developer based in Chile ("we", "us", or "our"). We are the publisher of the Android application CrummyStock (package name com.krisoll.crummystock).

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact us at krisoll@gmail.com.

Because CrummyStock stores data on your device and does not operate a cloud service, we generally cannot access, read, export, or delete the data inside your installation. We can still help you with instructions and, where applicable, with any personal data you send us directly (for example, an email support request).

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the CrummyStock Android app distributed through Google Play and as a direct APK, and to the privacy practices of the app itself. It does not apply to:

  • Google Play, Android, or your device manufacturer's own services and settings;
  • third-party apps or websites you open from the app;
  • files you create with other tools after exporting them from CrummyStock.

This policy is written for a general audience, including shop owners and staff. The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

3. Data stored on your device

CrummyStock stores the information you enter or capture so the app can work. This information is kept in the app's private storage on your Android device. Depending on how you use the app, it may include:

CategoryExamplesWhy it is stored

4. Permissions and device features

CrummyStock requests only the Android permissions needed for features you choose to use:

Camera

Used to scan barcodes and to take product or startup-screen photos. Barcode frames are processed on the device to read the code; they are not uploaded to us or to a server. Photos you take are saved in the app's private storage and may be included in a backup if you create one. You can deny camera permission and still use manual entry for products and sales.

Vibration

Used for optional haptic feedback when scanning. It is a normal Android permission and does not access personal data.

Internet, network state, and local network

Used only for the optional backup transfer feature, which lets you send or receive a backup between CrummyStock on your phone and another device on the same local Wi-Fi network. The app does not connect to a remote CrummyStock server, does not upload your data to us, and does not run a background server. On newer Android versions, the app also requests the local-network permission when you open the transfer screen. You can deny it and continue using the rest of the app.

The app does not request broad access to your files, contacts, location, microphone, SMS, or call logs. When you export or import a file, Android's system file picker lets you choose the location and grants access only to the file you selected.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit to ourselves, or maintain on a server:

  • your shop inventory, sales, suppliers, materials, promotions, notes, or photos;
  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you send them to us in a support message);
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • your contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, or microphone audio;
  • advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data;
  • analytics, behavioural profiles, or crash reports.

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no social-media SDK. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use that only to answer you and to handle your request, and we keep it only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law.

6. How data can leave the app

Data leaves the app only when you take an action that you choose:

Backup export

You can export a ZIP backup containing your shop data and images. The file is saved wherever you choose through Android's file picker. You can then store it, copy it, or share it with others using other apps. Once a backup leaves CrummyStock, its protection depends on where you put it and who can access it.

CSV and Excel reports

You can export sales reports as CSV or Excel files. These contain sales information and are saved where you choose. They are not uploaded to us.

Same-Wi-Fi backup transfer

The transfer feature sends a backup directly between two devices on the same local network. The data is not routed through our servers. The receiving device and the network are chosen by you, so you should use a trusted network and confirm that the receiving device is the correct one. The transfer server is active only while the transfer screen is open.

Android system backup

Android may include app data in the device's system backup if you have enabled backup in your device settings. That backup is controlled by Google and your device manufacturer under their own terms and privacy policies, not by us. You can turn it off in Android settings.

Email support

If you contact us, your email provider and ours process the message. We do not attach your shop data unless you choose to send it.

7. Backups may contain personal data

Important: CrummyStock backup files are ordinary ZIP archives. The app does not add a password or encryption to them. Anyone who obtains a backup file may be able to read the shop data, images, supplier details, notes, and sales records inside it.

Backups can include supplier names, phone numbers, addresses, notes, and other free-text information about people. If you export, copy, upload, email, or transfer a backup, you are responsible for:

  • keeping the file in a secure location;
  • sharing it only with people who are authorised to receive it;
  • deleting copies you no longer need;
  • complying with the data-protection and confidentiality obligations that apply to your business.

If you need encrypted backups, encrypt the ZIP file with a reputable tool before storing or sharing it, and keep the password separately.

8. Third-party components

CrummyStock uses a small number of third-party components. None of them is used by us to collect your shop data.

Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning

The barcode scanner uses Google ML Kit. The scanning is designed to run on the device. CrummyStock does not send your camera images or scanned barcodes to us or to Google. Google may collect limited technical or usage information through its SDK as described in Google's own terms and privacy policy. See ML Kit Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play and Android

If you install CrummyStock from Google Play, Google may collect information about the installation, updates, device, and app usage as part of its store services. If you use Android's system backup, Google or your device manufacturer may process app data under its own policy. We do not control those services. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Local libraries

The app also uses open-source libraries for the database, image loading, animation, and user interface. These libraries run locally and do not send your data to us.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics providers, crash-reporting services, data brokers, or social-media tracking SDKs.

9. Legal bases and your rights in Chile

Because CrummyStock does not operate a server and does not collect your shop data, most processing happens on your own device under your control. Where we do process personal data (for example, when you email us for support), we do so to respond to your request, with your consent, or for our legitimate interest in providing support and operating the app.

We aim to comply with applicable Chilean data-protection law, including Law No. 19.628 on the protection of private life and, when it enters into force, Law No. 21.719 on the protection of personal data.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete or cancel data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • request portability of data you provided;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with the competent Chilean data-protection authority.

For anything you sent to us by email, contact krisoll@gmail.com and we will help. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. 10. Retention

Shop data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Backup files and CSV/Excel exports remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. Android system backups follow your device's backup settings and Google's retention rules.

If you email us, we keep the message and your email address only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the request, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

11. Security

CrummyStock relies on Android's app sandbox to keep its private data separate from other apps. The security of that data also depends on your device: use a screen lock, keep Android updated, and avoid installing apps from untrusted sources.

Backups are not encrypted by the app. Treat every backup and export as sensitive. Use a secure location, restrict access, and delete copies you no longer need. For local Wi-Fi transfers, use a trusted network and close the transfer screen when you are done.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, especially for files you copy, share, or store outside the app.

12. Children

CrummyStock is a business tool for shop management and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the app does not require an account and does not send shop data to us, we do not knowingly maintain personal data about children on our systems. If you believe a child has sent personal data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International transfers

We do not transfer your shop data internationally because we do not receive it. If you email us, your message may be processed by email providers in other countries under their own safeguards. Google's services (Play, ML Kit, and Android backup) may process data in other countries under Google's policies.

14. Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete all app data: Android Settings → Apps → CrummyStock → Storage → Clear data. This removes the app's local database, photos, and settings.
  • Uninstall the app: removes the app and its private data from the device, subject to any Android system backup.
  • Delete backups and exports: delete the files from the folder, cloud drive, or device where you saved them.
  • Revoke permissions: Android Settings → Apps → CrummyStock → Permissions.
  • Turn off Android backup: use your device's system backup settings.
  • Stop a transfer: close the transfer screen; the local server stops.
  • Contact us: email krisoll@gmail.com for help with any personal data you sent us directly.

Deleting data from the app does not delete copies you made in backups, exports, emails, or cloud storage. You must delete those separately.

15. Changes to this policy

If a change is material, we will provide additional notice in the app or in the store listing where required. The version in effect when you use the app applies to that use.

12. Menores de edad

CrummyStock es una herramienta de negocio para la gestión de tiendas y no está dirigida a menores de edad. No recopilamos a sabiendas datos personales de menores. Como la app no requiere cuenta y no envía los datos del negocio a nosotros, no mantenemos a sabiendas datos personales de menores en nuestros sistemas. Si crees que un menor nos envió datos personales, contáctanos y los borraremos.

13. Transferencias internacionales

No transferimos los datos de tu negocio internacionalmente porque no los recibimos. Si nos envías un correo, tu mensaje podría ser procesado por proveedores de correo en otros países bajo sus propias garantías. Los servicios de Google (Play, ML Kit y copia de seguridad de Android) podrían procesar datos en otros países bajo las políticas de Google.

14. Tus opciones y cómo borrar datos

  • Borrar todos los datos de la app: Ajustes de Android → Apps → CrummyStock → Almacenamiento → Borrar datos. Esto elimina la base de datos local, las fotos y los ajustes.
  • Desinstalar la app: elimina la app y sus datos privados del dispositivo, salvo la copia de seguridad del sistema Android.
  • Borrar copias y exportaciones: elimina los archivos de la carpeta, unidad en la nube o dispositivo donde los guardaste.
  • Revocar permisos: Ajustes de Android → Apps → CrummyStock → Permisos.
  • Desactivar la copia de seguridad de Android: usa los ajustes de respaldo del sistema.
  • Detener una transferencia: cierra la pantalla de transferencia; el servidor local se detiene.
  • Contactarnos: escribe a krisoll@gmail.com para ayuda con cualquier dato personal que nos hayas enviado directamente.

Borrar datos de la app no borra las copias que hiciste en archivos de respaldo, exportaciones, correos o almacenamiento en la nube. Debes borrarlas por separado.
